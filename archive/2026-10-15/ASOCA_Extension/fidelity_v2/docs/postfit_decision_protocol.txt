GTA coronary development: decisions after the current fixed-budget runs
2026-10-14. This records the next decision; it does not authorize a changed
running objective, fivefold evaluation, or a claim of new model performance.

Evidence to collect
  GTA and common-recipe U-Net each use 25,000 segmentation updates on the same
  internal28, with the same four reserved patients. Read the verified fixed-final
  result and the 20-point internal-four trajectory. Retain every patient's Dice,
  pooled physical-mm ASSD, empty-prediction/undefined-distance status, and the
  recorded structural diagnostics. An undefined distance prevents a complete-
  cohort ASSD mean; it must not disappear from a comparison.
  Keep training losses, effective lambda, learning-rate groups and gradients
  beside the validation observations. Epoch51 activates auxiliary losses and
  partial classifier training; epoch71 expands the trainable classifier blocks
  while keeping those loss coefficients. Interpret these changes through the
  separate losses and stages. Compare segmentation loss separately; even its
  magnitude is not directly matched to U-Net because GTA adds anatomy weights.

Comparison and reporting
  Compare fixed-final GTA and U-Net on identical patient identities and grids.
  Record individual paired differences and cohort descriptive means, with clear
  direction (Dice larger, ASSD smaller). Four patients are development evidence;
  their patient standard deviation is not a fivefold standard deviation.
  GTA-versus-U-Net includes architecture/capacity, airway-backbone initialization
  and the independent 5,120-update classifier pretraining. It does not isolate
  the effect of the prior. Preserve these compute and initialization differences.
  The literature ASOCA values and the previously exposed original-eight results
  remain separate reference columns with their own splits, recipes and budgets.
  No pooling of internal-four and original-eight means, or substitution of old
  patient variability for a literature fold variability, is allowed.

When more training is a useful next experiment
  Inspect several late checkpoints and all four patients. Continued late Dice
  improvement together with reduced ASSD, after the full joint stage has had
  time to train, supports testing a longer budget. A decreasing training loss
  with a flat/worse validation trajectory alone does not support extending it.
  If late checkpoint improvements trade Dice against ASSD or depend on one
  patient, preserve that uncertainty rather than declaring convergence or gain.
  Any longer run needs an explicit new budget, source/config identity, parent
  weight/optimizer and patient provenance, and accounted additional updates.
  The current strict resume policy does not permit silently changing 200 to400
  epochs in the existing run directory or restarting its schedule unrecorded.

When changing structural regularization is useful
  Use the independent fixed training-patch diagnostic after resource and input
  checks. Compare actual foreground/background/shell MSE with the constant0.5
  and bank-optimal constant baselines; inspect Gaussian gradient energy in
  common and margin coordinates. The bank's CT/label/field/prior contents must
  match across checkpoint comparisons. This bank is not a validation/test set.
  A weak all-logit norm is not a measured full-network effect. A large common-
  mode energy can fit the auxiliary field without directly changing foreground
  probability; it is a possible mechanism requiring measured diagnostics.
  Increasing lambda scales a fixed derivative but does not necessarily improve
  its useful direction, sparse branch supervision, optimizer update or metrics.
  Change the late coefficient only as a separately recorded candidate justified
  by these observations. A changed target normalization or region-weighted MSE
  must be described as a changed loss, not as the unchanged historical recipe.

Minimum attribution control
  The current full GTA allocation can be compared with itself at Gaussian
  lambda10 versus0, retaining the same source, initial tensors, seeds, sampler,
  DS, anatomy weights, classifier checkpoint/budget and staged training. Keep
  the Gaussian module allocated in both arms. This isolates the regularization
  contribution conditional on the rest of this recipe; module-removal controls
  and a U-Net comparison answer broader questions. The existing notes describe
  how to verify identical initialization before any such run.

Classifier role
  Its fixed-final pretraining serves multitask stability and segmentation.
  Classification accuracy and confusion tables remain descriptive analyses.
  They do not select whether GTA is allowed to run and cannot replace Dice/ASSD.

Current stopping point for an automatic evaluator
  The current controllers finish their verified internal-four curves and stop.
  They do not launch longer training, increase lambda, select on original eight,
  or schedule fivefold studies. The active goal resumes scientific decisions
  from the resulting evidence; completion of a controller is not completion of
  the AEI research objective.
