GTA fidelity development: comparison boundaries and minimal attribution experiments

The current PlainUNet3D and GTA backbone are different implementations. PlainUNet3D
does not accept the historical SegAirwayModel backbone checkpoint. Consequently,
the common Dice+CE / sampler / patch / optimizer recipe calibrates training practice,
but GTA-versus-U-Net performance also includes the historical BAS backbone warm-start,
5,120 independent coronary-classifier updates, conditional modules, fusion, anatomy
reweighting and Gaussian regularization. That difference cannot all be attributed
to anatomical priors. Historical BAS pretraining costs remain separate/unknown;
25,000 segmentation updates alone do not make total compute matched.

The calibration has one frozen outer fold. New runs train on the same internal28,
with four reserved cases Diseased_10/Diseased_20/Normal_12/Normal_3. Their Dice and
pooled physical-mm ASSD are development diagnostics; clDice is auxiliary. The
original eight have already been inspected and are development data, not a blind
test. Never combine four-case and eight-case means as if they were one comparison.
The parent reports the existing native nnUNet100x250 result on the original eight:
Dice81.4748%, ASSD2.48754mm. Preserve those original source-backed files and budget;
this record does not remeasure them or claim reproduction of a1000-epoch pipeline.

Minimum follow-up for Gaussian attribution (already expressible by frozen code):
Use method=gta for both arms, identical BAS backbone checkpoint, identical completed
ASOCA classifier fixed80-epoch final.pt checkpoint, seed, internal28/4, full architecture,
decoder DS, freeze50/partial70/joint schedule, image-only FOV, standard sampler,
segmentation objective, anatomy weights, optimizer and25,000 segmentation updates.
Change only --gaussian-weight10 versus --gaussian-weight0; disable any late ramp.
Keep the Gaussian module allocated in both arms. Verify identical shared tensors
before the first update and record both source and initialization SHA256s.
This tests the Gaussian regularization contribution conditional on the chosen prior
and recipe. Prefer this to method=gta_no_gaussian for exact initialization matching:
removing gau_final changes parameter creation order before randomly initialized DS.
Gradient probes then distinguish scalar loss size from actual logits derivatives.

Minimum follow-up for classifier-conditioning attribution:
First keep the same SegAirwayModel backbone and its exact warm-start, the same
fixed-final classifier checkpoint/pretraining budget, and the same full GTA network allocation.
A separately versioned, audited conditioning-off switch should bypass all three
guidance injections and replace the16-channel guidance map by zeros while retaining
the22-channel final-fusion input. Preserve all common initial tensors, DS heads,
sampling, anatomy weighting, Gaussian lambda and segmentation schedule. Define in
advance whether independent classifier auxiliary training is retained in both arms
(to test conditioning alone) or removed (to test the whole classifier subsystem).
These are different estimands; neither should be labelled simply prior benefit.
Such a switch is a future minimal training-path change, not implemented or launched
by this controller, and must obtain a new source identity rather than edit frozen code.

The currently supported gta_no_prior is a useful module-removal ablation, with the
same BAS backbone initialization. It removes the classifier/guidance modules and
changes final fusion from22 to6 input channels. It therefore measures that combined
module removal and cannot provide exact whole-network initialization matching.
Setting classifier_weight0 alone does not turn off conditioning: the classifier
features, predictions and guidance map still enter segmentation. Do not substitute
that flag for a conditioning-off experiment.

For either follow-up, choose settings using internal diagnostics, retain fixed-final
checkpoint reporting, show individual patient results and paired differences, and
report selection history and extra pretraining/inference cost. Existing outer-eight
results may be described only as previously exposed development diagnostics. These
minimum recommendations do not authorize or schedule five-fold runs or new external
method reproductions. The initial old-server plan comprised the U-Net smoke,
common-recipe training and internal-four checkpoint trajectory; its later use is
recorded in the dated continuation below.

Classifier role correction: independent pretraining primarily stabilizes multitask
convergence. Classification attributes are training-derived structural priors serving
segmentation; classification scores are reference analyses. Main initialization uses
fixed80-epoch final.pt. The BA-selected best_selection.pt is a diagnostic artifact
and does not decide whether main training may start. Only completeness/source/patient
isolation/finite-readiness checks precede GTA; Dice and ASSD judge its performance.
